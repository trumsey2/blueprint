Paint Protection Film

An invisible, self-healing shield for your paint. Your vehicle isn't just protected, it's preserved.

Book a consultation Build your coverage

A clear layer of
armor

Paint Protection Film is a transparent, high-performance urethane film applied to your vehicle's painted surfaces to protect them from rock chips, scratches, bug acids, road debris and environmental damage.

At Blueprint Auto Care, we install professional-grade PPF designed to protect your investment and keep your vehicle looking new for years.
